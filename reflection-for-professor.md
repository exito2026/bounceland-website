# Student Reflection: English, Development Workflow, and Use of AI

## English communication

I communicate my goals clearly, even when I make spelling mistakes. Short, specific instructions—such as explaining that the old catalog was only for a preview—help guide the work effectively. I can continue improving my spelling, grammar, and word choice as I practice.

## Development workflow

My development workflow is practical and iterative. I share real business information, review the results, and correct anything that does not represent my business. I can improve by deciding key details earlier, such as whether the website should show four packages or the six packages in the old catalog. Before presenting the site as final, I should confirm the prices, package details, and WhatsApp number.

## Strategic use of AI

I use AI as a collaborator rather than accepting every suggestion automatically. I provide materials, make decisions, review the results, and guide the work. I treat AI-generated details as drafts until I verify them, especially when they affect customers.
